Tell us about your product ↗

Main image / white background

Clean up supplied real product photos while preserving shape, color, materials and included items. Main images need to meet the rules for the marketplace and category.

Secondary / infographics

Turn your verified dimensions, materials and product details into readable secondary images. No invented claims or unverified benefits.

Lifestyle / context

Show the product in a considered setting using accurate supplied product imagery. AI-assisted scenes are reviewed and must not misrepresent scale, use or features.

Common public guidance calls for a pure white main-image background and an accurate view of the item for sale, with promotional graphics kept off the main image. Requirements differ by category and marketplace and policies can change. Check the current official Amazon Seller Help image requirements before submitting. Approval is never guaranteed.
