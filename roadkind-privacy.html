Privacy, kept simple.

Effective September 25, 2026

What RoadKind does

RoadKind helps people in Canada prepare a safety-focused wildlife or roadside-animal report and find an appropriate official reporting channel. RoadKind is an independent routing tool and is not affiliated with a government agency or animal-welfare organization.

Information stored on your device

RoadKind can store report drafts, including any city, road, notes and coordinates you choose to add, in local storage on your device. An optional photo may be selected from your photo library for reference. RoadKind does not upload these drafts, coordinates or photos to a developer-operated server.

Location and photos

Location is requested only after you tap “Add location.” Photo-library access is requested only after you choose to add an optional photo. You may prepare a report without either permission. Never take a photo while driving or enter a roadway to collect information.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

RoadKind does not require an account and does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or developer-operated analytics.

Official third-party services

When you open an official website or phone number from RoadKind, you leave the app. Any information you then choose to submit is handled by that government, municipal or wildlife-health service under its own privacy policy. RoadKind does not claim that opening a link means a report was submitted.

Data deletion

You can delete a saved draft inside the app. Removing the app also removes its locally stored drafts from that device, subject to your device backup settings.
