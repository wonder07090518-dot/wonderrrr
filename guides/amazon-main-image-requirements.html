Amazon main image requirements

The MAIN image is the first product image and appears in search. It should let a buyer identify exactly what is for sale. Secondary images can explain details and context, subject to the same accuracy requirements and your category rules.

Amazon’s rules change, and category / marketplace style guides take precedence. Always check the current official Seller Central help before uploading. We do not guarantee approval or sales.

Source: Amazon Product image requirements (G1881). This overview uses the official US requirements supplied for this guide; the live help page may require sign-in or JavaScript.

MAIN image checklist

  • Use a professional photograph of the actual product on pure white: RGB 255, 255, 255. Graphics, illustrations, mockups and placeholders do not qualify.
  • Make the product occupy at least 85% of the image while showing the entire item. Keep it clear of the frame and avoid cutting it off; the help notes a jewelry exception.
  • Show one view of the product. Leave out excluded accessories and props that could confuse a buyer about what is included.
  • Keep added text, logos, borders, color blocks, watermarks and graphics off the image. Preserve the actual product rather than adding promotional overlays.
  • Photograph outside the packaging unless the box, bag or case is an important product feature.

Pixel sizes and file formats

There is no single required square export size. Amazon distinguishes basic display from zoom quality:

  • 500 px on the longest side is the site minimum.
  • 1000 px on the longest side is the minimum for zoom.
  • 1600 px or more on the longest side is recommended for optimal zoom.
  • Do not exceed 10,000 px on the longest side.

Accepted formats are JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg), TIFF (.tif), PNG (.png) and GIF (.gif); JPEG is preferred and animated GIFs are unsupported. Avoid blur, pixelation and jagged edges.

What belongs in other images?

Use secondary images for verified dimensions, close-ups and accurate lifestyle context where your category allows them. A dimension panel or multi-view layout should stay off MAIN. Across images, accurately represent the product and match the product title; the official general guidance also specifies at least 85% product fill.

Every detail page needs at least one product image. Amazon has recommended six images and one video, but plan useful views rather than repeating images to fill slots. Never add Amazon trademarks or lookalike badges such as Amazon’s Choice or Best Seller.

Check the category before export

Wonder Lab focuses on physical products such as home accessories and decor. Other categories can have specific posing and presentation rules, so a general checklist cannot replace the relevant marketplace style guide. Prohibited sexual content and special rules for images of children also apply.

A non-compliant image can remove a listing from search until it is corrected. When several selling partners supply images, uploading yours does not ensure Amazon will display it.
