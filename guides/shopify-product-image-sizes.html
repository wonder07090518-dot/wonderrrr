Shopify product image sizes

Shopify product images appear in your online store and Shopify POS. Choose an export size within the product-media limits, then check how your theme crops and displays it.

Policies can change. Verify the current Shopify Product media types help and any guidance for your item or theme before uploading. We do not guarantee approval or sales.

Product and collection image limits

  • Up to 5000 × 5000 px, or 25 megapixels.
  • Files must be smaller than 20 MB.
  • For square product images, 2048 × 2048 px usually displays best; it is a recommendation, not a universal requirement.

Shopify recommends PNG for most product images, followed by JPEG. TIFF, BMP, GIF, SVG, HEIC and WebP are also accepted, including animated GIF and WebP. Shopify’s Imagery service delivers a format supported by the customer’s browser.

Keep featured images consistent

Use a consistent aspect ratio for featured or main images so collection grids look even. Shopify creates multiple image sizes automatically, and themes can request consistent sizing through the Shopify CDN. Test the product page, collection grid and mobile crop in your chosen theme.

Shopify does not require a white background. Choose a background that keeps the product clear and accurately represented. Keep framing, lighting and scale consistent across a set.

Product media versus general uploads

The limits above belong to the Product media types page and apply to product and collection images. General Files and theme-image upload guidance discusses a separate 20-megapixel / 20 MB ceiling and directs readers to product-specific limits. Do not substitute that 20-megapixel figure for the 25-megapixel product-media limit.

See Uploading and managing files for general Files rules. Your theme’s banner or section dimensions are a placement decision, not a new universal product-image limit.

Plan a useful media set

The Adding product media guide sets a maximum of 250 images, 3D models or videos per product. You do not need to fill that allowance: start with a clear featured photo, useful angles, readable details and verified scale information.

Keep high-resolution originals and check that export compression preserves labels and texture. Use the source-photo checklist before sending materials to the studio.
