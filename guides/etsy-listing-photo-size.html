Etsy listing photo size guide

Prepare a clear first image, then use supporting photos to show details, scale and condition. Resolution helps buyers inspect an item, but it does not replace an accurate photograph.

Policies can change. Verify the current Etsy image requirements and best practices and any guidance for your item or theme before uploading. We do not guarantee approval or sales.

Listing photo dimensions

  • Etsy recommends at least 2000 px in both width and height for listing photos.
  • The first photo should be at least 635 px in both width and height to avoid lower placement in search. This is a floor, not the recommended working size or a ranking guarantee.
  • Files above 1 MB may fail to finish uploading, especially on slower connections. Keep high-resolution originals and compress an export if uploads stall.

The official listing creation guide allows up to 20 photos and two videos of 5–15 seconds. Its 2000-pixel-wide prompt is a recommendation; you can still publish when that suggestion is not met.

Plan for thumbnail crops

Use a landscape or square first photo. Its shape influences the later photos, so keep framing consistent. Leave space around the product for square, portrait and landscape thumbnails, then use Etsy’s thumbnail placement tool and inspect the result before publishing.

For a step-by-step crop check, see Etsy thumbnail cropping. Keep labels and important details away from the edges. If colors look wrong, convert the export to sRGB; Etsy converts images to sRGB, while CMYK is intended for print.

Supported files

Etsy accepts .jpg, .gif, .png, .svg and .heic only. Animated GIFs and transparent PNGs are unsupported; transparent areas appear black. Export an opaque background and check the uploaded result.

Keep an authentic photo

Most listings need at least one unedited photograph of the actual item against a real background, including a light box or white backdrop. Cropping, resizing, watermarking and file-format changes are allowed for that photo. Item-specific exceptions cover cases such as print-on-demand, digital downloads, customized or made-to-order items, and patterns or recipes. Check Etsy’s explanation of listing image requirements for your item type.

Other photos may use editing tools, including AI, for lighting, color or background changes, provided they remain accurate. Do not hide flaws or damage or change the item’s apparent color, condition, scale or materials. Every photo must reflect what the buyer receives.

The first image need not be the unedited photo, but should be your clearest, most realistic representation of the item. Keep an untouched original so you can distinguish it from edited exports.

Shop artwork has different sizes

A listing photo is not a banner template. Use the Etsy shop banner size guide when preparing shop identity artwork.
