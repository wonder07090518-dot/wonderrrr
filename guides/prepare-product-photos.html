How to prepare product photos for listing images

This is Wonder Lab studio guidance, not a platform rulebook. Good source photos help us preserve the product’s real shape, color and materials while preparing a clear listing-image set.

Before you photograph

  • Write down exactly what is sold: quantity, variant, color and included accessories. Separate styling props from the items a buyer receives.
  • Clean the product and lens. Use soft, even light and avoid mixed lighting that changes the apparent color.
  • Choose a simple background and steady the camera. Avoid strong filters, portrait blur and digital zoom that remove edges or texture.

Capture the details

  • Photograph the full item with space around the edges, then capture front, back, sides and useful close-ups.
  • Keep labels, packaging text, closures and important surfaces readable. Include honest views of condition and any flaws.
  • Show packaging separately and explain whether it is included or an important feature.
  • Provide verified dimensions with units, materials and supported claims for infographics. A ruler in a reference shot can help, but do not ask us to infer exact measurements from perspective.

Share originals and a clear brief

  • Send high-resolution original files through a shareable photo link, rather than screenshots or images compressed by messaging apps.
  • Keep an untouched set and label edited versions clearly. For Etsy, preserve the real-background photo needed for most listings.
  • Tell us the platform, marketplace, category, target placements and any theme crop requirements.
  • Share only materials you have permission to use. Supply verified product details; we do not invent features, certifications or benefits.

Check the destination specifications

Review Amazon MAIN image requirements, Etsy listing photo sizes or Shopify product image sizes. Check the current official platform help before uploading; policies and category guidance can change.

Send your materials

Send an inquiry with your photo link and product brief, or explore our Amazon, Etsy and Shopify image services. AI may assist backgrounds and layout, followed by human review. Real product photos remain the source of truth; we do not misrepresent products or guarantee platform approval or sales.
