Etsy shop banner sizes

Choose the banner placement before designing. Mini and big banners have different proportions; carousel and collage options require Etsy Plus. All dimensions below are width × height in pixels.

Policies can change. Verify the current Etsy image size help and any guidance for your item or theme before uploading. We do not guarantee approval or sales.

Prepare artwork for the placement

As studio guidance, keep essential text away from edges, use short readable wording and preview the shop on a phone and desktop. A wide banner is a poor place for tiny product specifications. Keep original artwork so you can adjust its crop later.

Use Etsy-supported .jpg, .gif, .png, .svg or .heic files. Animated GIFs and transparent PNGs are not supported; transparent areas appear black. Check the uploaded preview rather than assuming transparency will blend with the shop background.

For product photographs, use the separate Etsy listing photo guide, including its authenticity and thumbnail advice.
