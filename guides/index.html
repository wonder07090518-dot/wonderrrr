Listing image guides

Start with the platform and placement you are preparing. A search thumbnail, a product detail image and a shop banner need different crops and serve different purposes. These guides separate official specifications from studio preparation advice so you can make a useful brief.

Choose a guide

  • How to get a clean white background product photo — A practical phone photography workflow for a clean white background, natural edges and accurate product details.
  • What to put on Amazon infographic (secondary) images — Plan clear Amazon secondary images with readable callouts, verified measurements and substantiated claims.
  • Etsy thumbnail cropping: keep your product visible in search — Frame and test an Etsy first photo so the product remains visible when a search or grid thumbnail is cropped.
  • Lifestyle product photos for small shops on a budget — Create useful lifestyle product photos with simple props, natural light and accurate context on a small budget.
  • Amazon main image requirements — MAIN versus secondary images, white backgrounds, framing and zoom sizes.
  • Etsy listing photo size — resolution, thumbnails, file formats and truthful editing.
  • Etsy shop banner sizes — mini, big, carousel and collage layouts, plus shop identity images.
  • Shopify product image sizes — product and collection limits, consistent ratios and theme checks.
  • Prepare product photos — a practical checklist for originals, labels, dimensions and included items.

Use specifications as a starting point

Confirm the current official help, choose the image’s purpose, then check its crop at small screen sizes. Extra pixels cannot fix an inaccurate product view or a misleading claim. Keep original photographs alongside edited exports.
