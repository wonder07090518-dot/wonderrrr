THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How do I pay?

This is an inquiry only, not a payment. If we accept your project, Wonder Lab will send a PayPal invoice (Goods & Services). Work starts only after payment is confirmed. Please do not pay using Friends & Family.

When will I see a preview?

Expect a first preview in about 48 hours after payment is confirmed and usable photos and a complete brief are received. Complex items and schedules are agreed by email before invoicing.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is one consolidated round of changes within the agreed brief. Standard includes one round; Complete includes two. Trial includes no revision round. New concepts, extra images or changes to the product require a new quote.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI may assist background creation, layout exploration and lifestyle scenes, followed by human review. We use your real product photos as the source of truth. We do not invent features, change the product’s appearance or present generated imagery as an actual product photograph. Main images must meet your platform and category rules.

What if I need a refund?

Before work starts, contact us to request cancellation and a refund. Once work begins, completed work is not refundable; we will discuss any unstarted portion and problems with the agreed deliverables. Applicable consumer rights and PayPal dispute rights are unaffected.

What do you work on?

We focus on physical products such as home accessories and decor. We do not provide on-site photography, store management, website builds, standalone copywriting, medical claims or drug promotions. We do not promise sales, conversions or platform approval.

What should I send?

Send a product URL or name, accurate product details, your target platform and a shareable photo link. Only share material you have permission to use. We can arrange further photo sharing by email.
