FrameMap Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. This policy describes FrameMap version 1.0 for iPhone.

Data collection

FrameMap 1.0 does not create user accounts, show advertising, run third-party analytics, or send a user's saved photo-spot submissions to a FrameMap server.

Location

Location access is optional and is used to centre the map on the device. FrameMap does not store precise location on a FrameMap server. Map display and place search use Apple's MapKit services and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Pending submissions

Details entered in “Submit a photo spot” are saved locally on the device as pending review. They are not publicly posted or shared with other users in version 1.0. Removing the app removes this local data.

Seed location data

The bundled catalogue contains public place names, approximate coordinates and source identifiers. It does not contain user profiles or personal contact information.

Children and safety

FrameMap does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A location listing does not guarantee access, safety, or legal permission for photography or drone operation.

Changes and contact

If a future version introduces online community submissions, accounts or analytics, this policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before collection begins. Questions or deletion requests may be sent to wonder07090518@gmail.com.
