This policy explains what Wonder Ad Lab collects when you use our website or iOS app, why we use it, and how you can control your information.

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, optional WeChat ID, creative brief, service selections, feedback, order and top-up records, and reference files you upload. Account passwords are stored as salted password hashes, not as readable passwords.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and manage your account, process and deliver design orders, show order status and balance, send service and payment guidance, respond to revision requests, prevent misuse, and provide support.

3. Reference files and AI

Reference files are stored in private storage and are available only to authorized studio administrators handling the order. They are not displayed publicly. Our current upload flow does not automatically send reference files to an external AI model. If a future service needs external AI processing, we will disclose that use before the relevant processing.

4. Service providers and transfers

We use service providers for website hosting, private file storage, data storage, and transactional email delivery. These providers process information only to operate the service. Because the service is available internationally, information may be processed in countries outside your own, subject to the providers' security and privacy protections.

5. Payments

Wonder Ad Lab currently provides manual WeChat Pay or Alipay payment guidance and an account-balance option. We do not store bank card numbers in the website or app. A payment is not treated as confirmed until it is checked through the applicable official payment or bank record.

6. Storage, security, and retention

We use necessary session cookies and local app or browser storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, handle disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal or accounting obligations. No online system is completely risk-free, but we use access controls and private storage for customer files.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can sign out at any time. The iOS app includes an account deletion control under Account. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by emailing wonder07090518@gmail.com from your account email. We may retain limited transaction records where required by law, but they will no longer be used as an active customer account.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Material changes will be explained in the app or website where appropriate.
