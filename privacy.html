Privacy, with care.

This policy explains how Wonder Lab uses information on this website. Each app’s own support and privacy pages remain available through the Apps links below.

Inquiry details

We collect your email, optional name, platform, package choice, product URL or name, notes and optional photo link. We use these to assess your request, reply, agree scope and prepare a PayPal invoice if the project is accepted. Do not send sensitive information or private credentials in a photo link.

Email and payment providers

Inquiry notifications are delivered to wonder07090518@gmail.com through Resend, and a confirmation is sent to your email. Email providers process those messages to deliver our correspondence. If a project is accepted, PayPal processes the invoice and payment under its own privacy terms. We do not process bank cards on this website or store card numbers. Submitting an inquiry is not a payment.

Security and analytics

For abuse prevention, the inquiry endpoint temporarily uses the submitting IP address in server memory to limit repeated submissions. Hosting providers may process request logs under their own policies. Our existing aggregate analytics count visits and countries and respect browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. We do not save or display visitor IP addresses in our analytics. No advertising cookies are used by this new marketing site.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry correspondence only as needed to respond, perform agreed work and meet record-keeping obligations. Ask us to access, correct or delete your inquiry information by emailing the studio. Limited invoice or transaction records may need to be retained where legally required. Your photos and product rights remain yours; you must have permission to share the materials.

Existing app accounts

Existing App support, account deletion and Apple purchase services continue separately from this inquiry website. Where an app provides Account deletion, use that control or contact us from your account email. Consult your app’s privacy policy for its data practices.
