FrameMap Support

Discover photography viewpoints, plan the light and lens, and save a new photo spot for review — all from a fast, map-first interface.

Finding a photo spot

Move or zoom the map to reveal nearby pins. Tap a pin for its category, suggested time and lens. Search can also find places using Apple Maps.

Submitting a spot

Move the map so its centre is on the location, tap +, and add the spot details. Version 1.0 stores submissions on your device as pending review; it does not publish them online.

Access and safety

A map pin never guarantees legal access, safe conditions, or permission to fly a drone. Check current property, park, wildlife, airspace and local rules before visiting or flying.
