Civic Snapshot Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy describes Civic Snapshot version 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Data collection

Civic Snapshot does not create user accounts, show advertising, use third-party analytics, or send saved reports to a Civic Snapshot server.

Photos and report details

Photos, locations, descriptions, statuses and reference numbers are stored locally on the device. The app opens the official municipality's reporting channel, where the user decides what to submit under that authority's privacy practices.

No background submission

Civic Snapshot does not submit reports in the background. It shows the intended official authority before the user continues.

Government independence

Civic Snapshot is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by a municipality or government.

Retention and deletion

Saved cases remain on the device until the user deletes them or removes the app. Information submitted to an official service is governed by that service.
