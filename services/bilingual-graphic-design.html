COMMON QUESTIONS

Before layout begins

Is professional translation included?

The standard service designs client-supplied or client-approved text. Translation, rewriting or specialist terminology review must be confirmed as a separate scope.

Which language should appear first?

You decide based on the primary audience. Include that choice in the brief so the typography and reading order support it.

Can you adapt an existing design?

Yes. Upload the editable file or highest-quality export, approved bilingual copy, fonts and brand rules. We confirm feasibility before production.
