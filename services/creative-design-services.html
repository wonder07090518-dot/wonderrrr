COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you place an order

Can I order in English?

Yes. Choose English on the home page and write the entire brief in English. Order confirmations and the interface support both English and Chinese.

Can I upload reference files?

Yes. The order form accepts multiple files or a folder, up to 100 files and 1 GB in total. Uploaded references are stored privately for order processing.

How are final prices confirmed?

The service page shows the starting or fixed price for a defined scope. Extra formats, complex compositing, large copy changes and editable source files are confirmed before production.
