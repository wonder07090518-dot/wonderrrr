Civic Snapshot Support

Prepare a clear non-emergency civic report, see the responsible authority, and continue to its official reporting channel.

Prepare a case

Select an issue, attach one relevant photo, add the location and describe what you observed. Avoid photographing private personal information.

Submit officially

Review the destination, then open the listed municipality's official channel. Civic Snapshot does not submit on your behalf.

Track the result

After official submission, paste the real reference number into the case. Prepared, submitted and resolved remain separate statuses.

Safety and contact

Do not use the app for emergencies or immediate danger; call 911. For support or corrections, email wonder07090518@gmail.com.
