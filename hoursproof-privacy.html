HoursProof Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy describes HoursProof version 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Data collection

HoursProof does not create user accounts, show advertising, use third-party analytics, or send volunteer records to an HoursProof server.

Records and signatures

Volunteer activities, supervisor details, acknowledgement details and signatures are stored locally on the device. They leave the device only when the user chooses to export or share a report using Apple's system sharing tools.

No identity documents

HoursProof does not request or upload government identification, student cards or other identity documents.

School review

HoursProof helps organize evidence. It does not decide whether an activity is eligible or approved, and it is not affiliated with a school board or government.

Retention and deletion

Draft records may be deleted in the app. Removing the app removes its locally stored data. Exported files are controlled by the user and by the destination they choose.
