Help for using RoadKind across every Canadian province and territory.

Immediate danger

Call 911 only when human life is in immediate danger. Never stop in a live traffic lane, walk onto a highway, approach an injured animal or take a photo while driving.

Prepared is not submitted

RoadKind prepares a summary and opens an official reporting channel. Your report is complete only when that service confirms receipt.

Across Canada

Select your province or territory and road type. RoadKind will direct you to an official provincial, territorial, municipal or wildlife-health resource.
